Every owner deserves the full picture.

The problem

The owner of an Indian business runs blind between month-ends. Credit, stock, price and people are decided on memory and phone calls. The answers sit in the books, and nobody reads them in time.

What we built

NORTHLAMP is the owner’s command seat. It reads the books the business already keeps and shows who to chase, the cash four weeks ahead, where price is slipping and which clients are overdue for an order.

Why it wins

A small or mid-sized firm gets the view a finance team would give it, with nothing new to enter. A larger group gets every company and every salesperson in one seat.

Traction

In use at an early customer site. Trademark filed. Read-only by design: it never writes to the accounting software, and it runs on the owner’s own computer.

What’s next

  • Native iPad, iPhone and Android apps
  • A wider list of supported accounting software
  • An assistant that learns each business and suggests course corrections

The future

As the base grows, NORTHLAMP moves from showing the business to advising it, in the owner’s pocket and on the owner’s own data. Each business’s figures stay under that business’s control.
