Privacy

  • Runs on your PC. Your data sits in a file there.
  • Never writes to your accounting software.
  • Nothing from your books leaves your PC unless you use a feature that sends it: off-site backup to a cloud you choose, the optional AI assistant, sharing with your other laptops, web search, or lead search (sends a town name to a map service).
  • Internet features start shut on paid plans and trials, and normally on for a single-user Free install. You can switch them off in Settings.
  • Off-site backup and sharing run only after you set them up.
  • Apart from features you set up, it sends only a version check and a licence check-in, neither with figures from your books. Airplane mode blocks both.
