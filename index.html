Your whole business at your fingertips, on your own PC.

Your figures stay with you. Lock your book, and the book file is worth nothing to whoever copies it.

  • Reads on your device. Your books stay on your PC.
  • Before your books open, Windows asks if it is you: Windows Hello (face, fingerprint or PIN) or your password. It stays on unless you turn it off.
  • Lock your book and the book file is sealed with AES‑256. A copy will not open on another PC without two of your three recovery codes.
  • A new book is locked with AES‑256 from the first day, on every plan, and the copies the app keeps of it are locked too. If Windows on a PC will not keep the key to the lock, the app says so.
  • Lose the PC, keep the book: any two of your three recovery codes open it on a new one.
  • Sharing with your other laptops goes through a folder you choose. Notes are always encrypted; the laptops’ copy of the book is encrypted when your book is locked. You add each laptop; remove one and the key changes.
  • Every internet feature the app opens itself has a switch, and one switch stops them all. Folders synced by OneDrive or Google Drive are uploaded by that service, not by NORTHLAMP.
  • A log shows every connection the app itself has made, and every one it refused.
  • Only features you switch on send anything, apart from the weekly version check, the daily licence check and the WhatsApp buttons you press. Settings lists each one, what it sends and where. The version and licence checks carry no figures from your books.
  • On paid plans and trials, internet features start switched off, except the weekly version check, the daily licence check and the WhatsApp buttons, which open only when you press one.
